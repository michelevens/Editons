# Implementation Roadmap

## Milestone 0 — Audit
Inspect repository, dependencies, migrations, CI, shared components, deployment, and available reusable modules. Record decisions and test baseline.

## Milestone 1 — Manuscript core
Authentication, book projects, chapter tree, rich-text editor, autosave, persistence, version history, and basic import. Demonstrate round-trip without content loss.

## Milestone 2 — AI editorial review
Structured review jobs, issue panel, contextual suggestions, accept/reject, consistency indexing, research library, and citation provenance.

## Milestone 3 — Professional export
Edition templates, book layout, EPUB 3, print PDF, DOCX, validation reports, and realistic export fixtures.

## Milestone 4 — Collaboration and localization
Comments, role permissions, live editing, editorial workflow, glossary, French/Haitian Creole, and accessibility.

## Milestone 5 — Publishing business
Catalog, product pages, Stripe checkout, secure fulfillment, license tracking, royalty calculations, and analytics.

## Milestone 6 — Production hardening
Load testing, audit/security review, disaster recovery, cost controls, monitoring, and operational runbooks.

### Definition of done per milestone
Functional code, migrations, automated tests, lint/type checks, documentation, and demonstrable end-to-end scenarios. Mock screens alone do not count as feature completion.
