The studio

Everything a book goes through, in one place

Six stages, one manuscript. Each part saves, versions and exports the same text — there is no copy-and-paste between tools.

Write

A calm editor with the binder beside it.

  • Parts, chapters and sections you reorder by drag or by touch
  • Tables, images, footnotes, equations, charts and cross-references
  • Autosave with full version history and one-click restore
  • Word goals with progress rings, daily goal and streak
  • Typewriter scrolling and distraction-free mode

Review

Nine editorial agents, and you decide.

  • Developmental, line, copy, fact-checking, consistency, subject-matter and accessibility — plus master's and doctoral examiners for theses
  • Findings quote your sentence; review them one at a time, with undo
  • Suggest mode: tracked changes you accept or reject
  • Comments, @mentions, a review workflow and team roles

Research

Know what every claim rests on.

  • A research library where each source carries its verification standing
  • Citations in APA, Chicago, MLA, Harvard, IEEE or Vancouver, with a live bibliography
  • Drift detection when the cited sentence changes
  • Terms and names checked across the whole book at once

Design

Interior and cover, previewed as you go.

  • Trim size, margins, typeface, size and leading, running heads, drop caps
  • Cover Studio — or upload a finished cover from your designer
  • Every conventional book part, in the right order
  • Full-screen preview as a phone, an e-reader or a printed page

Publish

Files a store will take.

  • Reading PDF that opens on your cover, and a print-ready interior
  • EPUB 3, Word, Markdown and HTML
  • Exports build in the background and email you, cover included
  • ISBN & rights, editions, accessibility checks, store preparation

Grow

After the book, the readers.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does the AI rewrite my book?

No. Every finding is a suggestion you apply or dismiss, and an applied fix can be undone. Tracked changes from a co-author wait for your accept or reject too. Nothing changes your manuscript without your tap.

Can I really write on my phone?

Yes. The phone layout is the full studio: chapters, formatting on the keyboard, review, comments, design, cover, preview and export. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app. Tablets get the binder beside the text.

Which AI model reviews my chapters?

In early access the agents run built-in automated checks — long sentences, unsourced figures, mixed spelling, repeated words — clearly labelled “Sample” and free of credits. Model-backed reviews, with Claude among the providers, are switching on; the app will say which one ran every review.

Which languages can my book be in?

Any. The interface is in English; books in English, French, Kreyòl, Spanish, German and Portuguese get their book parts named in that language, and the Écrivain Prépa series above was made in French and Kreyòl.

Can I get my manuscript out?

Any time, as PDF, EPUB, Word, Markdown or HTML, depending on your plan — and Markdown on every plan. It is your book.

Is my unpublished manuscript used to train anything?

No. Text is sent to a review provider only when you run a review, and only the chapter being reviewed. Fonts are self-hosted in the studio, and the installed app keeps no copy of your manuscript on the device.

What happens if a review fails?

The credits are refunded automatically and the failure is shown as a failure — never as a clean chapter.

Is it finished?

Not entirely, and the “Honest status” section above says exactly which parts are coming. Writing, review, design and export work today.
