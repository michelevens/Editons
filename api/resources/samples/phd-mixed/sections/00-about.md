# About This Sample

This document is an illustrative sample doctoral dissertation supplied with Ecrivain so that graduate students can see how a complete mixed methods dissertation is structured, signposted and reported. It is not a real dissertation, it was not submitted for any degree, and it must not be submitted, in whole or in part, as anyone's work.

Kestrel Bay University is a fictional institution. The author, Tobias K. Lindgren, and the committee members named on the title page are invented, as are the Northern Lakes region, its four school districts, its 31 secondary schools, the district dashboard and every teacher quoted in the text.

All data reported here, including the survey responses from 412 teachers, the platform logs, the statistical estimates and the interview excerpts from 24 teachers, are synthetic, created to show how an explanatory sequential study is reported. No participants were recruited and no research took place. The numbers are internally consistent but describe no real population.

The scholarly works cited are real and come from a fixed reading list. Citations are written as tokens that Ecrivain formats in the student's chosen style; this sample uses Chicago author-date. Claims attributed to cited authors are paraphrased summaries and should be checked against the original sources before being relied on. Where a claim would need support that the reading list does not provide, the text marks it as [source needed], which is itself a habit worth copying.

Use the sample to study structure, argument and reporting conventions, not as a source of evidence.
