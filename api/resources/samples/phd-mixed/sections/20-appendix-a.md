# Appendix A: Survey Instrument

This appendix reproduces the Phase 1 survey as it was presented to teachers, excluding the consent screen and the district notice. Construct labels and item codes did not appear in the online version; they are added here to match the analyses in Chapter 5. Agreement items were adapted from the Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology {{venkatesh2003}}, except the trust items, which were developed for this study from {{mayer1995}} and {{lee2004}} (see Section 4.5.3).

## Introduction screen

Thank you for taking part. This survey asks about your experience of the district dashboard, the online tool your district provides for viewing student learning data. There are no right or wrong answers, and we are as interested in hearing from teachers who rarely or never use the dashboard as from those who use it often. Your responses go only to the research team at Kestrel Bay University. No individual response will be shared with your district, school or colleagues.

## Section 1: Your views of the district dashboard

For each statement, please indicate how much you agree or disagree.
Response scale: 1 = Strongly disagree, 2 = Disagree, 3 = Neither agree nor disagree, 4 = Agree, 5 = Strongly agree.

**Performance expectancy (PE)**

| Code | Item |
|---|---|
| PE1 | I find the district dashboard useful in my teaching. |
| PE2 | Using the district dashboard helps me identify students who need support more quickly. |
| PE3 | Using the district dashboard improves the quality of my instructional decisions. |
| PE4 | Using the district dashboard makes it more likely that my students will meet their learning goals. |

**Effort expectancy (EE)**

| Code | Item |
|---|---|
| EE1 | Learning to use the district dashboard was easy for me. |
| EE2 | My interaction with the district dashboard is clear and understandable. |
| EE3 | I find it easy to get the district dashboard to show me the information I want. |
| EE4 | It is easy for me to become skillful at using the district dashboard. |

**Facilitating conditions (FC)**

| Code | Item |
|---|---|
| FC1 | I have the resources necessary to use the district dashboard. |
| FC2 | I have the knowledge necessary to use the district dashboard. |
| FC3 | The district dashboard is compatible with the other systems I use for teaching (for example, the gradebook and learning management system). |
| FC4 | A specific person or group is available to help me when I have difficulty with the district dashboard. |

## Section 2: The data in the dashboard

The next statements are about the data the district dashboard shows, rather than the tool itself. Response scale as above.

**Trust in dashboard data (TR)**

| Code | Item |
|---|---|
| TR1 | The data shown in the district dashboard are accurate. |
| TR2 | The district dashboard gives a complete picture of my students' learning. |
| TR3 | The data in the district dashboard are up to date when I need them. |
| TR4 | When the dashboard's at-risk flag identifies a student, I believe the flag is reasonable. |
| TR5 | I am willing to base instructional decisions on dashboard data without checking them against other sources. |

## Section 3: Your plans

Response scale as above.

**Behavioral intention (BI)**

| Code | Item |
|---|---|
| BI1 | I intend to use the district dashboard in the next month. |
| BI2 | I plan to use the district dashboard to inform my instruction this school year. |
| BI3 | I expect to use the district dashboard regularly for the rest of this school year. |

## Section 4: About you

These questions help us describe who took part. They are not used to identify you.

1. How do you describe your gender? (Female; Male; Another identity; Prefer not to say)
2. How many years have you taught, including this year? (number)
3. What is your main subject area? (Mathematics; English/language arts; Science; Social studies; World languages; Arts; Physical education/health; Career and technical education; Special education; Other)
4. Which grade levels do you teach this year? (Select all that apply: 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12)
5. In which district do you teach? (Harrow Lake; Pine Ridge; Westfield; Calder Valley)
6. About how many hours of training on the district dashboard have you received in total? (None; Less than 1 hour; 1–3 hours; 4–6 hours; More than 6 hours)

## Section 5: How you have used the dashboard

Thinking about the **past month**, how often have you done each of the following? If you have not done something, please choose "Never" rather than leaving it blank.
Response scale: 0 = Never, 1 = Once, 2 = Two or three times, 3 = About once a week, 4 = Several times a week, 5 = Daily or almost daily.

**Use (USE)**

| Code | Item |
|---|---|
| USE1 | Viewed the class overview page for one of my classes. |
| USE2 | Viewed an individual student's page. |
| USE3 | Reviewed the at-risk flags for my students. |
| USE4 | Looked at item-level results on a common assessment (for example, which questions most students got wrong). |
| USE5 | Used information from the dashboard to form student groups or plan reteaching. |
| USE6 | Discussed information from the dashboard with a colleague or in a meeting (for example, a PLC meeting). |

*Note.* The use score is the mean of USE1 to USE6 (range 0–5).

## Section 6: Log data and follow-up

**Log data consent.** With your permission, your district will send the research team a count of how many times you logged in to the district dashboard during the past month. The count will be linked to your answers using a study code, not your name. Your district will not see your survey answers. You can take part in the survey without agreeing to this.

- Yes, I agree to my monthly login count being shared with the research team.
- No, I do not agree.

**Follow-up interview.** Later this year we will invite a small number of teachers to a confidential interview of about an hour, by video call, about their experience of the dashboard. Would you be willing to be contacted?

- Yes (please give an email address at which we may contact you; it will be stored separately from your answers)
- No

A closing screen thanked respondents and linked to a separate form for the gift card draw, so that contact details could not be linked to responses.
