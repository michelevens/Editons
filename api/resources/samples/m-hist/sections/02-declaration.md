# Declaration

I, Clara M. Ostrander, declare that this thesis, submitted in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the degree of Master of Arts in History at Kestrel Bay University, is my own work. It has not been submitted for a degree at this or any other institution. Where I have drawn on the work of others, published or unpublished, it is acknowledged in the footnotes and bibliography. The oral-history interview cited here was recorded with the narrator's informed consent under the procedures of the Kestrel Bay Oral History Project. Archival material is reproduced with the permission of the Kestrel Bay Historical Society.

Clara M. Ostrander
