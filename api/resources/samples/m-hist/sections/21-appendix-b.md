# Appendix B: Membership and Lending, 1928–1941

This appendix presents the two quantitative series on which Chapters 2 to 5 draw. Table B.1 gives the union's year-end position and its annual lending; Table B.2 gives the stated purposes of loans, grouped into the three periods used throughout the thesis.

*Note.* Members, shares and loans outstanding are taken from the treasurer's annual reports, KBTCU Records, box 6, and are rounded to the nearest dollar as reported. Loans made in each year are counted from the loan register, boxes 4–5; they sum to 2,516 for 1929–1941. Delinquency is the number of loans more than two months behind on scheduled repayment at year-end, as a percentage of loans outstanding by number, as reported by the treasurer. Losses written off totaled $1,284 over 1928–1941, of which $812 fell in 1932–1934.

*Note.* Purposes are coded from the treasurer's entries in the loan register using the rules described in Appendix A; an entry naming two purposes is assigned to the first. Percentages are rounded to whole numbers. Mean loan size is the total sum lent in each period divided by the number of loans made. The same purpose shares appear in Table 5.1, and the mean loan sizes in Figure 5.1.

Two cautions apply to both tables. The year-end figures are snapshots and do not capture movements within the year, which were large in 1932–1933, when the warrant scheme and the withdrawal limit altered the flow of shares from month to month. And the change of hand in the loan register in 1939 means that purpose and occupational entries for 1939–1941 are less detailed than for earlier years, although the count of loans and their sums are unaffected.
