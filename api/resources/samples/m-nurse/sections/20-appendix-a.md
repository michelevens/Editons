# Appendix A: Rounding Script and Log

This appendix reproduces the purposeful rounding script in the shortened form adopted in PDSA 2 and carried on a laminated pocket card, together with the fields recorded at each round. During PDSA 1 and PDSA 2 these fields were recorded on a paper log kept at the patient's door; from PDSA 3 (October 2024) the same fields were recorded in an electronic health record flowsheet. Rounds took place hourly from 06:00 to 22:00 and every two hours from 22:00 to 06:00, alternating between the registered nurse (RN) and the nursing assistant (NA) assigned to the patient.

## Rounding script (pocket card)

1. **Introduce and explain.** Knock, greet the patient by name, introduce yourself and your role, and say that you are checking in as part of hourly rounds.
2. **Pain.** Ask about pain or discomfort. If the patient reports new or uncontrolled pain, an NA notifies the RN before leaving the room; an RN assesses and treats.
3. **Position.** Ask whether the patient is comfortable and help to reposition if needed, following any turning schedule.
4. **Potty (toileting).** Offer help to the bathroom, commode or urinal. For every patient with a Morse Fall Scale score of 45 or above, offer toileting proactively at each daytime round and at each overnight round when the patient is awake.
5. **Possessions.** Place the call light, phone, water, glasses and any other items the patient uses within easy reach, and confirm that the patient can reach the call light.
6. **Safety check.** Confirm that the bed or chair alarm is on where indicated, the bed is low and locked, non-slip socks are on, the path to the bathroom is clear and the room is adequately lit.
7. **Closing question.** Ask: "Is there anything else I can do for you? I have time."
8. **Tell the patient when you will return.** State when the next round will take place and remind the patient to use the call light rather than get up alone.
9. **Document.** Record the round in the log (PDSA 1 and 2) or flowsheet (PDSA 3) before moving to the next room.

Overnight (22:00 to 06:00), sleeping patients are observed from the doorway without waking them unless they are due for scheduled toileting or care; the round is documented as completed with the patient asleep. An NA calls the RN before leaving the room if the patient reports new or worsening pain, appears newly confused or agitated, has attempted to get up alone, reports dizziness, or has had a near-miss since the last round.

## Rounding log fields

**Table A.1** Fields recorded at each round on the rounding log and flowsheet.

| Field | Entry | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Date and time | Time the round was completed | Hourly 06:00–22:00; two-hourly 22:00–06:00 |
| Room and bed | Room and bed number | No patient name on the door log |
| Rounder | Initials and role (RN or NA) | RN and NA alternate rounds |
| Morse ≥45 flag | Yes / No | From the most recent Morse Fall Scale score |
| Pain | Addressed / No pain / RN notified | |
| Position | Repositioned / Comfortable | |
| Toileting | Offered and accepted / Offered and declined / Not offered (asleep) | Proactive offer required when Morse ≥45 |
| Possessions | Within reach (call light, phone, water, glasses) | Single check confirms all items |
| Alarm | On / Not indicated | Bed or chair alarm for high-risk patients |
| Environment | Clear / Corrected | Bed low and locked, path clear, lighting, socks |
| Patient asleep | Yes / No | Overnight rounds only |
| Escalation or comment | Free text | Near-misses, RN notified, patient requests |

*Note.* From PDSA 3, these fields formed a single flowsheet row in the electronic health record, which also served as the source for the monthly documentation audit described in Appendix B.
