# Appendix C: SQUIRE 2.0 Checklist Map

This report follows the SQUIRE 2.0 publication guidelines for quality improvement work {{ogrinc2016}}. Table C.1 maps each of the eighteen SQUIRE 2.0 items, paraphrased, to the part of this report that addresses it.

**Table C.1** SQUIRE 2.0 items and their location in this report.

| No. | SQUIRE 2.0 item (paraphrased) | Where addressed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title: identifies the work as an initiative to improve health care | Title page |
| 2 | Abstract: a structured summary of the work, giving background, local problem, methods, interventions, results and conclusions | Abstract |
| 3 | Problem description: the nature and significance of the local problem | Chapter 1 |
| 4 | Available knowledge: what is already known about the problem, including relevant previous studies | Chapter 1 (in brief); Chapter 2 |
| 5 | Rationale: the informal or formal frameworks, models, concepts or theories used to explain the problem and why the intervention was expected to work | Chapter 1; Chapter 2 (Model for Improvement and change management) |
| 6 | Specific aims: the purpose of the project and of the report | Chapter 1 (SMART aim) |
| 7 | Context: the contextual elements considered important at the outset | Chapter 3 (context of 5 West) |
| 8 | Intervention(s): the intervention in enough detail for others to reproduce it, and the team who delivered it | Chapter 3 (intervention; Table 3.1); Appendix A |
| 9 | Study of the intervention(s): the approach used to assess the impact of the intervention and whether the observed outcomes were due to it | Chapter 3 (PDSA design and study of the intervention) |
| 10 | Measures: the measures chosen, their operational definitions, and how completeness and accuracy of data were assessed | Chapter 3 (Table 3.2); Appendix B |
| 11 | Analysis: the qualitative and quantitative methods used to draw inferences, including methods for understanding variation | Chapter 3 (run-chart analysis; Equation (3.1)) |
| 12 | Ethical considerations: the ethical aspects of the work and how they were addressed, including formal ethics review | Chapter 3 (ethical considerations) |
| 13 | Results: the initial steps and evolution of the intervention, process measures, contextual elements, outcomes and unintended consequences, and missing data | Chapter 4 (Figure 4.1, Table 4.1, Figure 4.2, Table 4.2) |
| 14 | Summary: the key findings and their relevance to the rationale and aims, and the particular strengths of the project | Chapter 5 (Section 5.1) |
| 15 | Interpretation: the links between interventions and outcomes, comparison with other work, impact on people and systems, and costs and trade-offs | Chapter 5 (Section 5.2) |
| 16 | Limitations: limits to generalizability, factors that might have affected internal validity, and efforts made to minimize them | Chapter 5 (Section 5.3) |
| 17 | Conclusions: usefulness of the work, sustainability, potential for spread, implications for practice and suggested next steps | Chapter 6 |
| 18 | Funding: sources of funding or other support and the role of any funder | Acknowledgements |

*Note.* Item wording is paraphrased from the guideline; readers should consult the published checklist for the full item descriptions.
