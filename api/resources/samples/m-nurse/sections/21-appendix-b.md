# Appendix B: Measure Definitions

This appendix gives the operational definition of each measure in the project's family of measures (Table 3.2). Outcome measures describe the result the project aimed to change, process measures describe whether the intervention was carried out, and balancing measures check for unintended effects elsewhere in the system. All data were de-identified unit-level counts or aggregate survey results.

**Table B.1** Measure definitions.

| Measure | Type | Numerator | Denominator | Source | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Falls per 1,000 patient-days | Outcome | Number of falls on 5 West in the month (an unplanned descent to the floor, with or without injury, assisted or unassisted) × 1,000 | Patient-days on 5 West in the month (sum of midnight census) | Incident reporting system; hospital census | Monthly |
| Falls with injury per 1,000 patient-days | Outcome | Number of falls resulting in any documented injury on post-fall assessment × 1,000 | Patient-days on 5 West in the month | Incident reporting system; post-fall assessment; hospital census | Monthly |
| Rounding documented | Process | Audited patient-hours in which a completed round was documented | Audited patient-hours (40 randomly selected per month) | Paper rounding log (July–September 2024); EHR flowsheet (October 2024–March 2025) | Monthly |
| Fall characteristics | Outcome (stratification) | Falls that were unassisted and toileting-related; falls occurring 22:00–06:00; falls in patients with Morse ≥45 | All falls in the period | Incident reports; post-fall huddle records | Each period |
| Call-light activations per patient-day | Balancing | Number of call-light activations on 5 West | Patient-days on 5 West | Nurse call system report | Baseline and March 2025 |
| Time per rounding visit | Balancing | Minutes per rounding visit, reported as median and IQR | Rounding visits observed | Two-week time study | October 2024 |
| Perceived workload ("Rounding adds to my workload") | Balancing | Sum of RN responses (1 = strongly disagree to 5 = strongly agree), reported as mean | RNs responding (n = 41 pre; n = 38 post) | Anonymous staff survey | Pre and post |
| Knowledge of high-risk patients ("I know which patients are at high risk of falling") | Process | Sum of RN responses (1–5), reported as mean | RNs responding (n = 41 pre; n = 38 post) | Anonymous staff survey | Pre and post |
| Prompt staff response | Balancing | Patients giving a top-box response to "staff responded promptly" | Patients responding to the item | Internal patient-experience survey | Baseline and intervention period |
| Pressure injuries | Balancing | Number of hospital-acquired pressure injuries on 5 West | None (count per period) | Incident reporting system | Baseline year and intervention period |

*Note.* EHR = electronic health record; IQR = interquartile range. The fall rate is calculated as in Equation (3.1). Survey items used a five-point agreement scale.
