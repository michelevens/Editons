# Appendix A: Search Strategy

This appendix reports the full electronic search strategy for the systematic review and meta-analysis presented in Chapter 3 (Paper 1), in line with the PRISMA 2020 requirement to present the full search for at least one database {{page2021}}. All four databases were searched from inception to March 2024, with no language restriction at the search stage. The strategy was developed with the help of a subject librarian at Kestrel Bay University, piloted against a set of known eligible studies, and then translated for each database.

## Structure of the search

The search combined three concept blocks with the Boolean operator AND:

1. **Heat exposure**: terms for heat, hot weather, high temperature, heat stress and heat strain, and for named exposure indices such as the wet-bulb globe temperature {{budd2008}}.
2. **Outdoor or manual workers**: terms for occupational settings and occupations with substantial outdoor or heavy physical work, including agriculture, construction, mining, forestry, fishing and outdoor labour in general.
3. **Outcomes**: terms for occupational injury and accidents, and for kidney outcomes, including acute kidney injury, renal function, creatinine, estimated glomerular filtration rate and chronic kidney disease.

Within each block, controlled vocabulary (MeSH in MEDLINE, Emtree in Embase, CINAHL Headings in CINAHL) was combined with free-text terms using OR. Animal-only studies were excluded at the final step.

## Translation to other databases

The MEDLINE strategy was translated for Embase (Ovid) by mapping MeSH headings to the nearest Emtree terms (for example, "heat stress" and "occupational accident") and retaining the free-text lines unchanged. For CINAHL (EBSCO), CINAHL Headings were used with the MH field code and free-text terms were searched in the TI and AB fields. Web of Science Core Collection has no controlled vocabulary, so the three concept blocks were searched as free text in the Topic (TS) field, with the NEAR/3 proximity operator used to link heat terms with work terms where this improved precision without losing known eligible studies.

## Other sources

Reference lists of all included studies and of relevant reviews {{spector2019;flouris2018}} were screened by hand, and forward citation searching was carried out for included studies. These supplementary methods identified no additional eligible studies beyond those retrieved by the database searches.
