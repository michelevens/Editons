# Publications Arising from This Thesis

## Papers included in the thesis

1. Rao NS, Benedetti C, Mwangi E. Occupational heat exposure and acute kidney and injury outcomes in outdoor workers: a systematic review and meta-analysis. *Journal of Occupational Health Sciences*. 2024. Published. (Chapter 3)
2. Rao NS, Otieno J, Benedetti C, Mwangi E. Daily worksite heat exposure and occupational injury in agricultural and construction workers: a time-stratified case-crossover analysis. Under review. (Chapter 4)
3. Rao NS, Haddad L, Benedetti C, Mwangi E. A heat-stress management programme for construction crews: a mixed-methods feasibility study. In preparation. (Chapter 5)

## Conference presentations

1. Rao NS. Weather stations or worksites? Measuring heat where people work. Oral presentation, Kestrel Bay University School of Public Health Research Symposium, 2024.
2. Rao NS, Otieno J, Mwangi E. Piece-rate pay and heat-related injury: a case-crossover analysis. Oral presentation, Coastal Region Occupational Health Forum, 2025.
3. Rao NS, Haddad L. "The supervisor makes the weather": early lessons from a construction heat-stress pilot. Poster, Kestrel Bay University Graduate Research Conference, 2025.

## Other outputs

A plain-language summary of Paper 2 was prepared for the Coastal Region Workers' Compensation Board and the participating unions in 2025, and a two-page briefing on Paper 3 was returned to the participating crews and site managers after the 2025 field season.
