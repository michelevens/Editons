# Statement of Contribution

This thesis includes three papers written for publication in peer-reviewed journals, presented as Chapters 3, 4 and 5. Kestrel Bay University's regulations for a thesis by publication require the candidate to be the principal author of each paper and to state her contribution, and that of each co-author, in a form the co-authors have endorsed. Contributions are described below using the CRediT (Contributor Roles Taxonomy) categories, with an overall percentage estimate agreed between the candidate and co-authors. The percentages reflect intellectual and practical effort across the whole life of each paper, from conception to the version reproduced here; they are estimates rather than measurements.

The co-authors are Professor Elias Mwangi (primary supervisor), Dr Clara Benedetti (secondary supervisor), Dr Joseph Otieno (environmental data scientist, Paper 2) and Dr Leila Haddad (occupational health and safety practitioner, Paper 3). All four have signed the co-author consent forms held by the Graduate Research School.
