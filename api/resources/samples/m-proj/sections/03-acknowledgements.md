# Acknowledgements

My first thanks go to the community health workers of Lowmoor District who let me follow them through their working days. They let me watch over their shoulders, answered questions in the middle of a visit, and, more than once, corrected my assumptions with patience and humor. I am equally grateful to the twelve health workers who spent a long day working through tasks with a prototype that was not yet finished. They told me exactly where it failed them. Mrs. Adongo and Mr. Kiprop gave generously of their time as supervisors and showed me how follow-up looks from the district office.

I thank my supervisor, Dr. Ingrid Solberg, for steady guidance, for asking "what did you see?" before "what did you build?", and for reading more drafts than either of us expected. My thanks also go to the members of the Interaction Design Lab at Kestrel Bay University, whose critiques of the paper prototypes saved several weeks of wasted effort.

Finally, I thank my family, who put up with a dining table covered in sticky notes for most of a semester, and my partner, who tested every build on a phone older than either of us would admit to owning.
