# About This Sample

This thesis is an illustrative sample supplied with Ecrivain to show what a complete master's thesis in software engineering can look like from the title page to the appendices. It is not a real piece of research and must not be submitted, adapted for submission, or cited as evidence of anything.

Everything about the study itself is invented. Kestrel Bay University is a fictional institution. The author, Daniel K. Ferreira, and the supervisor, Dr. Anika Rao, do not exist. The case company, Lanternfish Systems, its three engineering teams (Atlas, Beacon and Cormorant), its products, its rollout of an AI coding assistant, and every engineer quoted under a pseudonym are fictional. All repository metrics, model estimates, survey responses and interview extracts are synthetic, created to show how such a study is reported. No participants were recruited and no company data were analyzed.

The works in the reference list, by contrast, are real published sources. They are cited only for what they are generally known to report, they are paraphrased rather than quoted, and no page numbers have been attached to claims drawn from them.

The sample demonstrates the IEEE numbered citation style; the structure of a convergent mixed-methods case study in software engineering; an interrupted time series model with numbered equations; chapter-numbered tables; data-driven charts; interview extracts as block quotations; and a joint display integrating both strands. Use it as a model of structure, not of content.
