# Appendix B: Interview Guide

The guide below was used for all eighteen semi-structured interviews, conducted by video call between September and October 2025. It was used flexibly: the order of questions was adapted to the conversation, and prompts were used only where an answer needed elaboration. Interviewees were repeatedly asked for concrete, recent episodes, such as a specific pull request, review or bug, rather than general opinions.

## B.1 Opening (about 5 minutes)

- Thank the participant; confirm consent to record; restate the right to stop or withdraw.
- Explain that the interview concerns the team's work, not the participant's performance, and that nothing said will be reported to the company in identifiable form.
- Could you describe your role in the team and the kind of code you usually work on?

## B.2 Block 1: Using the assistant (about 15 minutes)

1. Walk me through how you used the assistant last week. What were you working on?
   - Prompt: Which mode, inline completion or chat, did you use, and for what?
2. Think of a recent time the assistant helped you. What happened, step by step?
3. Think of a recent time it got in the way or produced something you had to fix. What happened?
4. Are there kinds of work where you deliberately do not use it? Why?
5. How do you decide whether to accept a suggestion?
   - Prompt: Does that depend on what the code does?

## B.3 Block 2: Writing, reviewing and testing (about 20 minutes)

6. Has the way you write code changed since March? In what ways?
7. Has the way you write tests changed?
   - Prompt: Who or what writes the tests now?
8. Thinking about pull requests you have reviewed recently, has reviewing changed?
   - Prompt: Can you tell when code was generated? Does it matter?
   - Prompt: Has the size or content of pull requests changed?
9. Have you seen problems, such as bugs, security findings or reverts, that you would connect with the assistant? Tell me about one.
10. Has the assistant changed how you learn, or how others on the team learn?
    - Prompt (for junior participants): How confident are you that you could write this code without it?
    - Prompt (for senior participants): Has it changed how you support newer colleagues?

## B.4 Block 3: The team (about 15 minutes)

11. Has your team agreed anything about how the assistant should be used, formally or informally?
    - Prompt: How did that come about? Who proposed it?
    - Prompt: Is it followed? By everyone?
12. If a new engineer joined your team tomorrow, what would you tell them about using the assistant here?
13. Do you think the assistant has affected your team differently from other teams in the company? Why might that be?

## B.5 Closing (about 5 minutes)

14. Is there anything we have not covered that you think matters?
15. Is there anything you said that you would prefer not to be quoted, even under a pseudonym?

- Thank the participant; explain the next steps and how to contact the researcher or withdraw.
