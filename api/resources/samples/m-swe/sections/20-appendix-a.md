# Appendix A: Survey Instrument

The survey was administered online in September 2025 to all 44 engineers in the Atlas, Beacon and Cormorant teams. It was anonymous and took about ten minutes to complete. The items are reproduced below in the order in which respondents saw them. Agreement items used a five-point scale: 1 = strongly disagree, 2 = disagree, 3 = neither agree nor disagree, 4 = agree, 5 = strongly agree. "The assistant" refers to the AI coding assistant introduced at the company on 3 March 2025, including both inline code completion and the chat panel.

## A.1 About you

1. Which team are you a member of? (Atlas / Beacon / Cormorant)
2. Which seniority band best describes your current role? (Junior / Mid-level / Senior / Staff or tech lead)
3. How long have you worked as a professional software engineer? (Less than 2 years / 2–5 years / 6–10 years / More than 10 years)

## A.2 How you use the assistant

4. In a typical working week since March 2025, how often have you used the assistant? (Daily / A few times a week / About once a week / Less than once a week / Never)
5. Which of the following have you used the assistant for? Select all that apply.
   - Boilerplate code and tests
   - Looking up unfamiliar APIs, libraries or languages
   - Refactoring existing code
   - Debugging
   - Writing SQL
   - Writing documentation or comments
   - Other (please describe)
6. Which mode do you use more often? (Mostly inline completion / Both about equally / Mostly chat / I do not use it)

## A.3 Your views on the assistant

Please indicate how far you agree with each statement.

7. The assistant makes me more productive.
8. I review assistant-generated code more carefully than code I wrote.
9. I trust suggestions for security-sensitive code.
10. The assistant helps me learn unfamiliar APIs or languages.
11. Reviewing teammates' PRs has become harder.
12. My team has clear expectations about how the assistant should be used.

## A.4 In your own words

13. Describe a recent occasion when the assistant saved you significant time. What were you working on?
14. Describe a recent occasion when the assistant's output caused a problem or needed substantial correction.
15. Has your team agreed any rules or habits about using the assistant, formally or informally? If so, what are they?
16. Is there anything else about the assistant and your work that you would like to tell us?

## A.5 Interview invitation

17. If you would be willing to take part in a follow-up interview of under an hour, please use the separate link shown on the next page. Your survey answers will not be linked to your interview.

*Note.* Results for items 4, 5 and 7–11 are reported in Chapter 4 (Table 4.3). Item 12 and the open-text items informed the interview guide (Appendix B) and the qualitative analysis but are not reported separately.
