# Appendix A: Interview Guide

This guide was used flexibly in semi-structured interviews lasting 60 to 75 minutes, conducted by video call. Questions were not always asked in this order or in this wording; follow-up prompts were used where a participant's answer opened a relevant line of discussion. Participants were told at the start that they could keep their camera off, skip any question and stop at any time.

## Opening (about 5 minutes)

Before recording began, I thanked the participant, confirmed that they had read the participant information sheet (Appendix B), answered any questions and confirmed verbal consent to record. I then reminded them that there were no right or wrong answers and that I was interested in their own experience.

## Part 1: Background and route into the programme (about 10 minutes)

1. Could you tell me a little about yourself and what you are studying at the moment?
2. What led you to choose a fully online master's programme?
3. You mentioned that neither of your parents completed a bachelor's degree. How, if at all, has that shaped your experience of university, either earlier or now?
   - *Prompt:* Was there anything about starting graduate study that felt familiar, or unfamiliar?

## Part 2: Experiences of belonging (about 20 minutes) — RQ1

4. When you think about your programme, are there moments when you have felt that you really belonged? Could you describe one?
5. Are there moments when you have felt that you did not belong, or that you were on the outside? What was happening?
6. What does "belonging" mean to you in an online programme? Is it different from what it would mean on campus?
   - *Prompt:* Who, if anyone, knows you in the programme? How do you know that they know you?
7. How do you usually take part in your courses: live sessions, discussion boards, other ways? How does each feel?

## Part 3: Programme practices and interactions (about 20 minutes) — RQ2

8. Can you think of something an instructor or the programme did that made you feel more part of things? And something that made you feel less part of things?
9. How clear have the expectations been, for example about assignments, referencing or contacting instructors?
   - *Prompt:* When you have not been sure about something, what did you do?
10. How do you feel about asking questions or asking for help in your courses?
11. Some programmes ask students to have cameras on in live sessions. What has your experience been?
12. If you could change one thing about how your programme is run, to help students like you feel they belong, what would it be?

## Part 4: Resources beyond the university (about 15 minutes) — RQ3

13. Who, outside the university, supports you in your studies? In what ways?
14. How do the people at home think about your degree? Do you ever have to explain it to them?
15. How does your work fit with your study? How does your employer view it?
16. Do you keep in touch with other students outside the course site, for example in a group chat? How did that start, and what is it like?
   - *Prompt:* Is there anyone who is not part of it?

## Closing (about 5 minutes)

17. Is there anything we have not talked about that you think is important for understanding your experience?
18. Is there anything you would like to ask me?

After the recording ended, I thanked the participant, explained the next steps (transcription, pseudonymization and an optional summary for member reflection) and confirmed the details for the $25 gift card.
