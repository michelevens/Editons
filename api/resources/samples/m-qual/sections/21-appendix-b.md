# Appendix B: Participant Information and Consent

The following information sheet and consent form were sent to prospective participants by email after they responded to the recruitment notice in their programme newsletter. The study was approved by the Kestrel Bay University Institutional Review Board (protocol KBU-IRB-2025-118).

## Participant information sheet

**Study title:** Belonging at a Distance: How First-Generation Students Experience Fully Online Master's Study

**Researcher:** Danielle R. Osei, MA in Higher Education candidate, School of Education, Kestrel Bay University

**Supervisor:** Dr. Miriam Achterberg, School of Education, Kestrel Bay University

**What is the study about?** This study explores how students who are the first in their family to complete a bachelor's degree experience belonging in fully online master's programmes, and what programmes do that helps or hinders that sense of belonging.

**Who can take part?** You can take part if you are currently enrolled in the fully online MBA, MS in Nursing Leadership or MA in Instructional Design at Riverside State University, are aged 18 or over, and neither of your parents or guardians holds a bachelor's degree.

**What will I be asked to do?** You will be invited to one interview by video call lasting between 60 and 75 minutes, at a time that suits you. You may keep your camera off. With your permission, the audio will be recorded and transcribed. After the analysis, you may be sent a short summary of the findings and invited to comment, if you wish.

**Do I have to take part?** No. Taking part is voluntary. Your decision will not affect your grades or standing in your programme. The researcher is not a member of staff in your programme. You may skip any question, stop the interview at any time, and withdraw your data up to four weeks after your interview without giving a reason.

**Are there risks or benefits?** Some questions ask about difficult experiences in your studies or at home. You can decline to answer or pause the interview. Contact details for student support services will be provided. There is no direct benefit to you, but the findings may help programmes support future students. You will receive a $25 electronic gift card as a thank-you, whether or not you complete the interview.

**How will my information be protected?** Recordings will be stored on encrypted, password-protected university storage and deleted once transcription is checked. Transcripts will use a pseudonym, and details that could identify you (such as your employer or the names of instructors) will be removed or altered. Only the researcher and supervisor will have access to the data. Anonymized transcripts will be kept securely for five years and then destroyed. Quotations may appear in the thesis and in any related publications, always under a pseudonym.

**Questions or concerns?** Please contact the researcher or supervisor by email. If you have concerns about how the study is conducted, you may contact the Kestrel Bay University IRB office, quoting protocol KBU-IRB-2025-118.
